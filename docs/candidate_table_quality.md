# Quality of the candidate positions

Written on 15 November 2026 and revised on 20 November 2026, as a complement to
[`data_quality_and_cleaning.md`](data_quality_and_cleaning.md), which is kept
exactly as submitted on 3 November 2026 and covers the official election files.

The second peer review noted that the candidate positions decide which finalist
each eliminated group is assigned to under H1 and H2, yet had no row in the
quality assessment. They are assessed here with the same three characteristics.
Figures are generated by `vote-transfer analyse` (`reports/results/numbers.json`,
`candidate_table_quality.json`); this document names the checks.

## Main: the poll-based scale

| Characteristic | Metric | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Each poll order matches its source report | 22 orders, each linked to its source. **[PENDING: poll orders checked against sources]** |
| Consistency | Agreement between institutes on a candidate's position (range between lowest and highest) | Above 0.4, the candidate is unplaced (Lassalle 2017). Largest range left for a placed candidate: about 0.36 (Lassalle 2022) |
| Completeness | Every candidacy in every poll of its election; every identifier in the Ministry files | Complete. Cheminade (2012, 2017) is set aside by rule, because institutes list him last by default |

**Response.** Unplaced candidates are removed and the scale is recomputed
without them; their voters follow the H0b row. These rules were fixed before any
model was run on the scale. 2002 rests on one institute (Ipsos), and the 2017 and
2022 orders come from a published compilation rather than the institutes' own
reports: both are stated as limitations.

## Comparison: the hand-coded 1–7 families

| Characteristic | Metric | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Review by two independent coders | Not done. The table was replaced as the main input instead (see `docs/project_scope.md`) |
| Consistency | The same party label carries the same family in every election | Flags the PS (Hamon 2017 coded differently) and the UDF (Bayrou 2002 and 2007), both documented choices |
| Completeness | Rows without a score | The two Cheminade records (2012, 2017) |

The table stays `draft` and is used only to show how much the results depend on
where candidates are placed (`reports/results/sensitivity.csv`). The main
conclusion is the same on both scales; H2 is much worse on the 1–7 scale.
